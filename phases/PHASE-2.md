# Phase 2: CPML

**Target: 2 weeks. Docs: 02-numerics (CPML, measuring reflection), 05-pitfalls.**

The phase that separates this project from every hobby wave simulator. Budget
for overrun; tuning against a measured number takes iterations.

## Scope

1. `cpml.rs`: graded profiles for sigma, kappa, alpha at integer and
   half-integer positions; `b`, `c`, `1/kappa` arrays per axis
2. PML terms merged into the single TMz update path using identity
   coefficients in the interior
3. Corner regions (both x and y stretching active)
4. Reflection measurement harness: reference-domain method, point source,
   probes along the PML face, reflection vs incidence angle
5. Parameter sweep tool: thickness, m, sigma scale, kappa_max, alpha_max
6. Line source validation against the Hankel function (now possible because
   the domain is open)

## Acceptance

- A9: reflection at or below -60 dB from normal incidence to 70 degrees with a
  10 cell layer
- A10 (target): -80 dB with a tuned layer of 16 or more cells. Record the best
  measured value and its parameters regardless of whether it reaches -80.
- A8: line source amplitude and phase within 2 percent of the Hankel solution
  beyond two wavelengths
- Late-time stability: 100000 steps with a source that stops early, energy
  decays monotonically to the round-off floor, no growth in the PML
- All Phase 1 tests still green, bit-for-bit unchanged in closed-cavity cases
  with PML thickness zero

## Do not

- Hand-tune by looking at pictures. Tune against the number.
- Move on with a first-order boundary "for now"
