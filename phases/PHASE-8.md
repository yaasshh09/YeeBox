# Phase 8: Instruments, presets, launch

**Target: 2 weeks. Docs: 06-experience, 04-validation (report).**

## Scope

1. Remaining instruments: spectrum, transmission with guided normalization,
   energy meter
2. Remaining tools: lens, rod lattice, ring, monitor line, far-field contour
3. Remaining presets from the experience doc, each with watch/try text and a
   validation link where one exists
4. Export: PNG, WebM clip, scene JSON
5. Performance pass: profile first, then act
6. Accessibility pass; reduced motion; keyboard access
7. README: what it is, the four validation plots, known limits, how to verify
8. A written devlog of the CPML tuning and the worst bug, from `PROGRESS.md`

## Acceptance

- Every preset loads, runs, and survives random painting for 60 seconds
  without instability
- Fuzz test: 500 random valid scenes, 2000 steps each on the CPU kernel, no
  growth beyond the source energy
- Page weight under 2 MB compressed
- Validation report regenerated by CI and linked from the app
- Known limits section present and accurate

## Done when

Someone who has never seen the project can open the link, play for a minute,
click Verify, and find the evidence.
